How to prepare for JAMB Chemistry

Preparation works best as a system: syllabus coverage, practice, review of mistakes, and rest.

Map the syllabus

List topics you have not touched in months.

Schedule weaker topics earlier in the week when attention is higher.

Practice under light time pressure

Untimed practice builds understanding.

Short timed sets build exam tempo. Use both.

Review is part of studying

Re-work missed questions after a day.

If you miss the same idea twice, rewrite the concept in your own words.
