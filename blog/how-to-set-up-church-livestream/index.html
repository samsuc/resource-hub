Church media

How to set up a church livestream

A church livestream works best when the plan is simple enough for a volunteer team to repeat every week.

Start with the service goals

Decide what the livestream must deliver: clear teaching audio, readable lyrics or scripture, and a stable picture. You do not need a broadcast studio on week one.

Write down who is responsible for audio, cameras, and the streaming computer. Unclear ownership is a common cause of last-minute stress.

Basic signal flow

Audio should come from the church mix (or a dedicated stream mix) into the computer used for streaming.

Video can start with one locked-off camera. Add a second camera only when the team can switch without losing audio focus.

Before you go live

Test internet upload speed at the same time of day as your service.

Confirm the stream key, scene layout, and backup plan if the network drops.

Run a short private test stream and watch a minute of the recording or preview.

Keep improving weekly

After service, note one audio issue and one video issue—not ten. Small weekly improvements compound.
