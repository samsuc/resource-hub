JAMB Chemistry revision

Effective JAMB Chemistry revision rewards consistency and active practice more than long passive reading sessions.

Build a weekly rhythm

Alternate concept review with timed practice questions.

Keep a short error log: topic, mistake type, and the correct idea in one sentence.

Prioritize high-yield understanding

Mole concept, periodicity, bonding, acids and bases, organic basics, and common reaction patterns appear often in exam-style practice.

Depth on core ideas beats shallow coverage of everything the night before.

Use short tools, not only textbooks

Formula sheets and condensed notes help during spaced review, especially when you already understand the underlying concept.
