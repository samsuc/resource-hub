OBS church livestream setup

OBS is flexible, which is useful—and easy to overcomplicate. A small, stable setup beats a complex one that only one person understands.

Keep the scene list short

A practical starting set: Widescreen / teaching, Wide / worship, Scripture or lyrics, and a simple standby slide.

Name scenes clearly so a substitute operator can find them under pressure.

Audio first

If people cannot hear the message, the stream fails even when the picture looks polished.

Prefer a clean feed from the audio desk when possible. Monitor levels during rehearsal, not only after the service starts.

Reduce failure points

Disable sleep on the streaming computer.

Close unused apps.

Save the OBS scene collection and export a backup to a second drive or cloud folder.
