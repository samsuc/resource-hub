Church Resources

Practical church media resources for livestream setup, media teams, and Sunday operations — built for volunteers and leads who need clarity under pressure.

Church livestream setup involves more than pressing go-live. Audio checks, camera positions, OBS scenes, and team roles all need to be consistent. SAMSUC Resource Hub provides church media resources that help teams prepare before service starts.

Whether you are building a church streaming setup for the first time or tightening an existing media workflow, these downloads focus on repeatable steps: what to check, who owns each task, and how to reduce avoidable technical issues during worship.

Media leads, OBS operators, camera volunteers, and church media team members can use these resources alongside your existing tools. Every product page lists what is included so your team knows what they are downloading.
