Church Media Operations Manual

Sample SOP excerpts for church media teams. Full manual unlocks after purchase.

Sample: Pre-service media checklist

  • Confirm stage mics and battery levels
  • Test switcher inputs (cameras 1–3)
  • Load slides / lyrics for service order
  • Verify livestream key and destination
  • Run a 30-second audio + video test recording

Sample: Role clarity

DirectorCalls camera switches and cues.
Audio leadManages levels, monitors feedback.
GraphicsAdvances slides and lower thirds.
Stream opWatches stream health and chat.
