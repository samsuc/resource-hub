Frequently asked questions

What happens after you buy — delivery, formats, license, and support.

You complete a secure checkout, then receive digital access to your files. There is no physical shipping — everything is delivered electronically.

After payment, download links are sent to the email you use at checkout. You can also re-download later from your purchase receipt link.

Website templates are HTML, CSS, and JavaScript files you open and edit locally. Study and church resources are digital documents (such as guides and printable checklists) delivered as downloadable files.

Portfolio and SaaS templates are built with clean HTML, CSS, and JavaScript so you can edit content, colors, and layout. Study sheets and church documents are ready-to-use files; you can print or adapt them for your own use as needed.

Portfolio and landing page templates may be used for personal and client projects. Redistributing or reselling the template source files themselves is not allowed. Full terms are included with the download where provided.

Where a product is marked for a specific year (for example 2026 exam materials), we aim to keep that content current. When an update is published, re-download from your original receipt link.

If a file won’t download or access fails, use the contact form on this site with your purchase email. We’ll help you resolve access issues quickly.

Because these are instant digital downloads, refunds are limited. If a file is corrupted or you cannot access what you paid for, contact us and we’ll work to fix the problem.
